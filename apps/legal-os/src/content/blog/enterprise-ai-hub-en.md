### Unified Billing: One Credit Pool, Infinite Possibilities

One of the most significant frustrations for operations managers is managing multiple billing cycles and unpredictable usage costs. Traditional SaaS models force you to pay for seats that might go unused in one department while another department faces overage charges.

Smartek solves this with [Unified Credit Billing](/en/pricing). You purchase a single pool of credits that can be spent anywhere across the platform. If your legal team has a quiet month but your clinical staff is overwhelmed, your resources shift automatically to where they are needed most. This "Pay-As-You-Go" flexibility ensures that every dollar spent is maximized for operational efficiency.

### Corporate Memory: Building an Institutional Brain

When a senior partner or a lead physician leaves a firm, they often take their institutional knowledge with them. This is the "brain drain" that haunts professional services.

Our Hub OS uses advanced **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**. You can build a [Private Knowledge Hub](/en) (your firm’s proprietary brain) that stores templates, precedents, and guidelines. You decide exactly who can access which department. A lawyer can see legal precedents, while a doctor can see clinical guidelines—all while drawing from the same underlying secure infrastructure. This ensures that your firm’s intelligence grows over time, creating a "Corporate Memory" that remains a permanent asset of the enterprise.
