### Introduction: The Human Cost of Clinical Documentation

In clinics across the globe, a quiet crisis is unfolding. It isn’t a lack of medical expertise, but a surplus of administrative burden. Doctors today spend nearly half of their workday staring at screens, navigating electronic health records, and documenting patient encounters. This "Pajama Time"—the hours spent finishing paperwork after the clinic closes—is the leading cause of physician burnout.

The solution isn't more staff; it's more intelligence. The rise of [Medical AI](/en/medical) is finally providing doctors with the tools they need to return their focus to where it belongs: the patient.

---

### What Exactly is an Intelligent Scribe?

An intelligent scribe is far more than a simple transcription tool. While traditional voice-to-text systems just record what is said, an [AI Medical Scribe](/en/medical) understands clinical context. It listens to the natural conversation between a doctor and patient, identifies relevant medical facts, and organizes them into structured documentation.

#### Does this replace my medical secretary?

No. Think of it as a highly specialized co-pilot. Your medical secretary manages the patient flow, scheduling, and office operations. The intelligent scribe, however, sits inside the consultation room (digitally), ensuring that every clinical detail is captured accurately. It removes the need for the doctor to type during the exam, allowing for better eye contact and a more empathetic patient experience.
